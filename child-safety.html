Zero-Tolerance Safety Policy

Child Safety Standards & CSAE Policy

Application: Vizura (Google Play & Web) • Effective Date: November 2026 • Child Safety Contact: info@vizura.app

01 Zero-Tolerance Commitment Against CSAE & CSAM

Strict Prohibition of Child Sexual Abuse and Exploitation (CSAE)

Vizura maintains an absolute, zero-tolerance policy against Child Sexual Abuse and Exploitation (CSAE) and Child Sexual Abuse Material (CSAM). Any content, account, message, or behavior that exploits, abuses, endangers, or sexualizes minors (individuals under the age of 18) is strictly prohibited across all Vizura mobile and web services.

These Child Safety Standards apply globally to every user, creator, and visitor of the Vizura mobile application (available on Google Play and iOS) and the Vizura web platform (vizura.app). Violations result in immediate content removal, permanent account termination, device/IP blocking, and mandatory reporting to relevant legal authorities.

02 Explicitly Prohibited Content & Conduct

Vizura strictly prohibits the creation, uploading, sharing, solicitation, storage, or distribution of any of the following across feeds, Vibes, Vids, Pics, Boards, profiles, comments, or direct messages:

  • Child Sexual Abuse Material (CSAM): Any visual depiction—including photographs, videos, illustrations, AI-generated or digitally altered imagery, or audio—of sexually explicit conduct involving anyone under 18 years of age.
  • Child Sexual Exploitation and Abuse (CSAE): Grooming, sextortion, sexual solicitation, coercion, trafficking, or any predatory interaction directed toward a minor.
  • Sexualization of Minors: Content that sexualizes, objectifies, or fetishizes minors, including suggestive captions, hashtags, or roleplay involving minors.
  • Facilitating or Linking to Abuse: Sharing external links, invites, contact details, or coded language intended to direct users to off-platform CSAM or CSAE networks.
  • Child Endangerment: Content encouraging self-harm, physical abuse, bullying, or dangerous behavior targeting children or teens.

03 Age Eligibility & Minor Protection

  • Minimum Age Requirement: Vizura is intended strictly for users aged 13 years and older (or 16 where required by regional law, such as under GDPR). Accounts suspected of belonging to children under the minimum age are immediately suspended and purged.
  • Privacy & Safety Controls: Users can block abusive accounts, control direct messaging interactions, mute accounts, and delete their accounts and personal data permanently at any time inside Profile → Settings → Account & Security.

04 In-App User Reporting & Feedback Mechanism

How to Report Safety Concerns Directly in Vizura

Every post, Vibe, Vid, comment, direct message, and user profile inside the Vizura app includes an accessible Report action so community members can flag suspected CSAE, CSAM, or child safety concerns immediately.

  • Report a Post, Vibe, or Video: Tap the options menu (•••) on any post or video and select Report, then choose the safety/abuse reason. Flagged items are prioritized for urgent Trust & Safety review.
  • Report a User or Direct Message: Open the user's profile or chat thread, tap the options menu, and select Report or Block to immediately sever contact and alert our moderation team.
  • Direct Escalation by Email: Anyone—including non-users, parents, guardians, and law enforcement—may report suspected child safety issues directly to our dedicated Child Safety Point of Contact at info@vizura.app with the subject line "URGENT: Child Safety Report".

05 Enforcement, Moderation & Removal

When Vizura obtains actual knowledge of suspected CSAM or CSAE—whether through in-app user reports, email escalations, or automated/manual safety reviews—our Trust & Safety team takes swift, decisive action:

  • Immediate Content Removal: Confirmed or suspected CSAM/CSAE material is immediately taken offline and removed from public and private feeds.
  • Permanent Account Termination: Accounts involved in producing, distributing, soliciting, or facilitating CSAE or CSAM are permanently banned without notice or appeal, and associated identifiers are blocked from re-registering.
  • Evidence Preservation: Relevant account records and metadata are securely preserved strictly as required by law for official law enforcement investigations.

06 Compliance with Child Safety Laws & Law Enforcement Reporting

Vizura complies with all applicable international, federal, and regional child safety laws and regulations, including 18 U.S.C. § 2258A and Google Play's Child Safety Standards Policy.

  • Mandatory Reporting to NCMEC & Authorities: Confirmed instances of CSAM or apparent CSAE are reported promptly to the National Center for Missing & Exploited Children (NCMEC) CyberTipline and/or appropriate regional and national law enforcement agencies.
  • Cooperation with Investigations: Vizura cooperates fully with authorized law enforcement requests and child protection organizations to protect minors and support investigations.

Child Safety Point of Contact

Vizura has designated a dedicated Child Safety Point of Contact authorized to review, escalate, and act on child safety compliance inquiries, Google Play policy notices, user safety reports, and law enforcement requests.
