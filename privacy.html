Privacy Policy

Effective Date: November 2026 • Contact: info@vizura.app • Coverage: Global (GDPR, CCPA/CPRA, Apple & Google Play Compliant)

01 Introduction & Scope

This Privacy Policy describes how Vizura ("we", "us", or "our") collects, uses, protects, and discloses personal data when you access or interact with the Vizura mobile application, services, and related digital products.

We are dedicated to safeguarding your digital identity and privacy through data minimization, state-of-the-art encryption, and transparent governance. By using Vizura, you consent to the data practices described in this policy.

02 Information We Collect

We collect only the minimum necessary information to deliver, protect, and enhance our services:

A. Information You Voluntarily Provide

  • Account Credentials: Username, profile display name, email address, and profile avatar provided during registration.
  • User-Generated Content: Creative boards, visual vibe posts, audio clips, custom reactions, bio descriptions, and chat messages.
  • Support Communications: Feedback, inquiries, or bug reports submitted to our support team.

B. Information Collected Automatically

  • Device & System Telemetry: Device manufacturer, model, operating system version, screen resolution, display refresh rate, and locale settings.
  • Hardware & Motion Sensor Data: Device sensor telemetry (such as gyroscope and accelerometer data) utilized strictly locally on your device to render dynamic parallax motion cards and 3D visual effects. Motion sensor data is never transmitted to our servers.
  • Usage & Performance Diagnostics: Aggregated feature interactions, error logs, and session diagnostic telemetry to resolve crashes and optimize application fluidness.

C. In-App Purchase & Billing Data

All financial transactions—including virtual coin pack purchases and premium subscription memberships—are processed exclusively by the official Apple App Store or Google Play Store. We do not collect, process, or store credit card numbers or bank account details. We only receive encrypted transaction tokens and digital receipt identifiers to credit entitlements to your account vault.

03 How We Use Your Data

We utilize your data under lawful legal bases (contract performance, legitimate interests, and user consent) for the following purposes:

  • To establish, authenticate, and maintain your account.
  • To display your public creative profile, published vibes, and social interactions to other community members.
  • To process digital purchases, track login bonus streaks, and credit in-app virtual currency.
  • To deliver optional, user-initiated rewarded video advertisements that award free in-app perks.
  • To detect, prevent, and remediate fraud, software abuse, security breaches, and community violations.
  • To comply with regulatory, accounting, and legal requirements.

04 Trusted Third-Party Infrastructure

We partner with top-tier, industry-standard third-party service providers who process data strictly under comprehensive Data Processing Agreements (DPAs):

  • Cloud Infrastructure & Database Hosting: Scalable, encrypted global cloud database hosting and storage services to secure user profiles and media assets.
  • In-App Purchase & Receipt Verification: Certified purchase validation services that verify receipt tokens with Apple and Google.
  • Product Analytics & Diagnostics: Privacy-conscious telemetry and session performance diagnostics tools to trace bugs, monitor frame rates, and improve user flows.
  • Digital Advertising & Attribution: Licensed ad networks and conversion attribution providers to deliver opt-in rewarded video ads and measure marketing campaign efficacy using standard privacy-safe advertising identifiers.

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal data to data brokers or unauthorized third parties under any circumstances.

05 Account Deletion & Data Retention

Right to Immediate Account & Data Deletion

You have the absolute, unconditional right to delete your account and all associated personal data at any time. We believe you should have complete ownership over your digital footprint.

You can permanently delete your account and erase your information through either of the following methods:

Method 1: Direct In-App Self-Service Deletion

  • Open the Vizura app and navigate to your Profile tab.
  • Tap the Settings icon in the top corner.
  • Scroll down to Account & Security and select Delete Account.
  • Confirm the deletion prompt. Your account will be immediately deactivated and scheduled for permanent purge.

Method 2: Direct Email Request

  • Send an email from your registered account address to: info@vizura.app with the subject line "Account Deletion Request".
  • Our data compliance team will verify your identity and process the complete erasure of your records within 48 business hours.

What Happens Upon Account Deletion:

  • Your profile, username, email address, and authentication credentials are permanently purged.
  • All uploaded creative pins, vibes, media files, and comments are permanently removed from public view.
  • Your unspent virtual currency balances and virtual inventory licenses are extinguished upon deletion.
  • Aggregated, anonymized system error logs that cannot be linked back to an individual are retained for ongoing security analytics.

06 Your Global Privacy Rights (GDPR & CCPA/CPRA)

Regardless of your geographic location, we extend full global privacy rights to all users:

  • Right to Access & Portability: You may request a machine-readable export of all personal data we hold about you.
  • Right to Rectification: You may correct or update inaccurate profile details directly within the app at any time.
  • Right to Erasure ("Right to Be Forgotten"): You may request the permanent deletion of your data as outlined in Section 5.
  • Right to Opt-Out of Advertising Telemetry: You can reset or disable your device Advertising Identifier (IDFA on iOS, GAID on Android) directly via your operating system privacy settings.
  • Non-Discrimination: We will never deny services, charge different prices, or degrade quality if you exercise your legal privacy rights.

07 Children's Privacy Protection

Vizura is strictly intended for individuals aged 13 years and older (or 16 in certain European jurisdictions). We do not knowingly collect, solicit, or maintain personal information from children under 13. If we discover that an account has been registered by a minor under 13, we will immediately terminate the account and purge all associated records.

If you believe a child under 13 has provided us with personal information, please contact us immediately at info@vizura.app.

08 Security & Data Safeguards

We deploy rigorous technical, organizational, and physical safeguards designed to prevent unauthorized access, disclosure, or loss of your personal information. All communication between the mobile app and cloud servers is enforced via modern Transport Layer Security (TLS 1.3) with encrypted storage at rest.
